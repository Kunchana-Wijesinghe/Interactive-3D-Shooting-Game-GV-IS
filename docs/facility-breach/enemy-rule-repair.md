## Implemented rules

- **Shared Ammo1 combat:** one pending shot, 50 rounds, reserve consumption and reload pause, configurable cadence, approximately .4-second white warning, .4 initial miss probability, and exactly 10 damage on a successful resolution. Range/physical LOS is checked again on resolution; shooter/target hierarchy is ignored while environmental solids remain blocking. Pause, enemy defeat, disabled attack components, player death, timeout and victory cancel pending attacks. Terminal player damage remains rejected. Diagnostic counters expose resolved shots and successful applications.
- **PG:** teal/60 HP, four corridor waypoints beside the card/waste, 13-unit sight, 110-degree FOV, continuous .5-second detection, 12-unit fire range, remembered-position investigation, four-second search and return to patrol. No hearing subscription. The new `PatrolGuardBrain` replaces the attached temporary adapter; its unused legacy source is retained.
- **SH:** orange/80 HP near exit; existing Perera Dijkstra/ranker/memory libraries remain unchanged. Exact acoustic membership and door attenuation still determine audibility; gunshots outrank cans, memories expire after ten gameplay seconds, and investigation follows emitted origin. Sight remains all-round/16 units; fire range is now 14. Disabling or losing sight cancels its pending attack, and revival clears its selected sound/search state. Door-opening requests stay inside valid movement routes.
- **CD:** violet/90 HP guarding Machinery/device. Valid visual perception supplies the last-known position. Every 1.5 seconds of valid sight, actual complete navigation paths and physical cover occlusion score guard-area candidates. Lost sight stops movement for a three-second hold, then returns to its post. It faces the device initially and keeps its body aimed at remembered perception while strafing toward cover. Other robots are not treated as environmental cover.
- **FL:** lime/70 HP in Machinery. Complete navigation paths qualify candidates 3?9 units from remembered position, at least 50 degrees off direct chase. On arrival it faces the remembered position and reacquires actual sight before shooting. Another plan requires at least two seconds and over four units of newly perceived movement; failed plans cannot retry every frame. After three seconds without sight it searches four seconds and returns. Hidden live player coordinates never feed cover/flank goals.
- **Defeat/revival:** sensing, movement requests, attacks and chest light stop at zero HP. Safe revival uses the actual enemy capsule dimensions, replacing the old undersized .3-radius query; retries are bounded to .5 gameplay seconds. Revival restores HP/magazine and resets movement, memory and detection state. Existing roster logic expires timers before permanent-defeat evaluation and permanently defeats all four when simultaneously temporarily inactive.
- **Mission:** existing five-minute/100-HP start, card gates, five-second shutdown with running countdown, timeout tie precedence, frozen-countdown unlimited escape, alive exit victory and loss at zero HP are retained. Kills do not gate the objective. Living robots can still attack during escape; victory cancels them.

Changed production components: `EnemyCombatAdapter`, `PlayerDamageAdapter`, `RoleDecisionBrain`, `PatrolGuardBrain`, `CoverDefenderBrain`, `FlankerBrain`, `AgentPerception`, `SoundHunterNavMeshMover`, `FacilityBreachSoundHunterBrain`, `EnemyPresentation`, and `FacilityBreachRuntime`.
